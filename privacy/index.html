Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This policy explains how Smeltara: A Smithy Odyssey (“Smeltara”, “the game”) and its developer, İzzet Nacar (“we”, “us”), handle information. Smeltara is a single-player game. You can play locally without creating an account.

1. Information stored on your device

Smeltara stores game progress and preferences in the app’s private device storage. This can include coins, gems, workshop progress, upgrades, equipment, reward receipts, timestamps, language, audio, haptics and graphics settings. This local data is used to run the game, restore progress after closing it and calculate offline rewards.

You can reset local progress from Settings → Start Over. Uninstalling the game normally removes its local app data, subject to your device platform’s backup behavior.

2. Test-version Cloud Save and account sign-in

Cloud Save and account sign-in are disabled in the current public release configuration. Some test versions provide these optional features through Unity Player Accounts, Unity Authentication and Unity Cloud Save, services provided by Unity Technologies. The following information applies if you used those test features.

  • Unity Player Accounts processes the email address you use to create or access the account and creates a unique Unity Player Account ID. Smeltara does not receive your password.
  • Unity Authentication creates a Player ID and authentication/session data used to recognize the signed-in player.
  • Unity Cloud Save processes the Authentication Player ID and IP address and stores the Smeltara save file and file metadata that you choose to back up.

We use this information only to authenticate you, compare device and cloud progress, back up your selected save, restore it on another installation and prevent accidental overwrites. Cloud Save data is not public and is not used to match you with other players.

Fictional invitations: guest-master names and “invited while away” thank-you messages are generated locally for gameplay. They are not real player profiles, do not come from another account and are not sent to another player.

3. Support email

If you contact us, we receive the email address and any text, screenshots or other information you choose to include. We use it to answer the request, diagnose the reported problem and keep a reasonable record of the conversation. Do not send passwords, payment information or other sensitive data. Nothing is sent merely by opening the support link; your email app sends only after you choose to send.

4. Store and website links

The game can open the applicable app marketplace, including the Apple App Store or Google Play, for ratings and this GitHub Pages website for support and legal information. Test versions with account features can also open Unity’s account portal. Those services process visits under their own policies:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • GitHub General Privacy Statement

This website contains no advertising, analytics, cookies set by İzzet Nacar or contact form.

5. Optional advertising and related data

Smeltara offers optional rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob. The game does not show forced advertisements. When an internet connection and applicable privacy eligibility allow it, the game may load advertisements at startup so a reward video is ready when you choose it. An advertisement is shown only when you select a rewarded-ad action. If an advertisement is unavailable, you can continue playing without it.

The current release uses restricted Google advertising. It does not ask for your age, date of birth or age sharing and does not request permission for cross-app tracking. It does not enable Unity Ads or AppLovin mediation, Firebase Analytics or Firebase Remote Config. In-app purchases and production account sign-in are disabled.

Google’s advertising and privacy services may process your IP address (including approximate location inferred from it), device or app identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. Google and its advertising partners process advertising-service data for ad delivery, reporting and fraud prevention under their policies. Restricted treatment limits advertising processing; it does not mean that no information leaves the device. Smeltara does not send its gameplay save file, cloud account details or a birth date to Google for advertising.

The game checks Google’s privacy eligibility without opening an automatic startup consent form. If the required eligibility is missing, advertisements remain unavailable. Where offered, use Settings → Privacy Choices to review Google’s privacy options.

Google handles its service data under the Google Privacy Policy. Google’s advertising services and partners are also described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Those pages explain applicable privacy controls, access, retention and deletion. Resetting Smeltara’s progress does not itself delete data already processed by Google’s advertising services.

We do not sell your gameplay save file or contact information. Advertising-service processing may be treated as sharing under some privacy laws; use the applicable privacy choices and provider controls described above.

When Smeltara shares information with a service provider, we require that provider, through its applicable terms and policies, to give the information the same or equivalent protection described in this policy and to process it only for the applicable purposes described above and as permitted by law.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Local game data remains until you reset it, clear app data or uninstall the game.
  • Test-version Smeltara Cloud Save data remains until you delete it. In a test version with Cloud Save, while signed in, choose Settings → Start Over → Device + Cloud to delete both Smeltara cloud save slots before resetting the device.
  • A Unity Player Account can be managed or deleted through Unity Player Accounts. Unity has the direct cross-application account relationship; deleting the Unity account is separate from deleting Smeltara’s cloud files.
  • Advertising-service data is handled under Google’s policies and privacy controls. Start Over does not withdraw advertising privacy choices or delete Google-held data.
  • You may also follow our data-deletion instructions or contact us for help.

7. Security

Local data uses the operating system’s app sandbox. Test-version cloud requests use Unity’s supported authentication and Cloud Save services. No system can be guaranteed perfectly secure, but we minimize the data used and do not put account passwords in the Smeltara save file.

8. Intended audience

Smeltara is intended for players aged 13 and over. The game does not collect a birth date or ask players to share their age. Restricted advertising is applied without deciding that any particular player is an adult.

Local play does not require an account. Test-version Unity Player Accounts and Cloud Save are not intended for a child below the digital-consent age that applies where they live unless a parent or guardian has provided any required consent. Such players should use local play only unless a parent or guardian manages the optional account sign-in. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information to us.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to ask about, access, correct or delete personal information. For Smeltara application data, use the in-game deletion route or email us. Use Google’s privacy controls for information processed by its advertising services. Test-version Unity Player Account access or deletion requests must be made directly through Unity’s account portal or its published privacy contact.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the game, service providers or legal requirements change. The date at the top identifies the current version.
