Support

Official support contact for Smeltara

For gameplay problems, reward-video questions, accessibility feedback or privacy requests, email İzzet Nacar. Nothing is sent automatically; the button opens your email application.

Email Smeltara support

Please include: the game version shown at the bottom of Settings, your device model, what you expected and what happened. Screenshots are useful. Never send a password, sign-in code or payment information.

Reward videos

If a reward video is unavailable, continue playing and try again later. Availability depends on connectivity, privacy eligibility and advertising supply. Include the game version and the affected reward action when reporting a problem. Do not send passwords or account identifiers.

Test-version Cloud Save

Cloud Save and account sign-in are disabled in the current public release configuration. The following guidance applies to test versions that provide those features.

If device and cloud progress differ, Smeltara shows both before changing either copy. Choose the progress you want to keep. If you are offline, continue locally and retry when connected.

Delete data or an account

Use the dedicated data-deletion instructions. Smeltara Cloud Save files and the cross-application Unity Player Account are separate.

Response

Support is handled by an independent developer. We will make a reasonable effort to reply, but response times are not guaranteed.
